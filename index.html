Broceries

  • iOS app
  • UX research
  • UI/UX design
  • Apple Developer Academy, 2025
View on the App Store

A grocery list for people who still forget the milk. Write it like you would on paper, shop in the order of the aisles and never lose last week's list.

Why it exists

It started when Sasa watched her mom prepare for every shopping trip and still forget things at home. She observed and interviewed shoppers before choosing a direction, and kept the app simple enough to use with one hand in an aisle.

What it does

  • Smart grocery list that groups items by supermarket section
  • Flexible input by typing, voice or scanning a receipt or note
  • Missed items carry over, and every finished list can be reused

Design choices

  • Dark green ground and high contrast text so adult and older eyes read it easily
  • Simple, fast interaction with low cognitive load
  • Honest limits: no price awareness yet, not built for complex household planning

About

From factory floors to Figma.

Sasa trained as an industrial engineer, with a focus on health and safety systems, then moved into UI/UX at Apple Developer Academy in 2025, designing apps from user research to final screens.

Today she builds brand systems and websites as Assistant Manager for Marketing at LearnLink China, coaches English speaking on Preply and hosts events as a master of ceremonies. Her work is clear first and cute second: research, a simple structure, then the illustrations that make it feel like hers.

She also designs to sell. Her coffee stickers, designed, printed and sold online, reached Rp 4 million in profit in their best month.
